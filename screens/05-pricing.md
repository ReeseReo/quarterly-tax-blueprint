# Screen 5: Pricing Page

**Route:** `/pricing`
**Purpose:** Convert free users to paid trials at an impulse-buy price.
**Emotional Target:** Confidence. "This is a no-brainer for $6/mo."
**Primary Action:** Tap "Start 14-Day Free Trial."

## Layout

Centered. Two-tier pricing as side-by-side cards (stack on mobile).

- **Headline:** "Simple Pricing. No Surprises." — 28px bold, centered.
- **Subheadline:** "The calculator is free forever. Pay only when you want more." — 16px muted.

### Free Card
White, standard border. $0.
Features: Quarterly calculator, federal + SE tax + QBI deduction, safe harbor guidance, state tax (as added).
CTA: "Use Free Calculator" (outlined green).

### Pro Card
Green border, "MOST POPULAR" badge. $6/mo with toggle to $49/yr ("Save 32%").
Everything in Free plus: expense tracking with IRS categories, receipt photo capture + storage, deadline reminders (email + push), quarterly history, tax preparer export (CSV/PDF).
CTA: "Start 14-Day Free Trial" (solid green).

### Below Cards
- **Money-back guarantee:** Shield icon. "30-day money-back guarantee."
- **Competitor anchor:** "QuickBooks Self-Employed: $15/mo. FreshBooks: $19/mo. FlyFin: $16.99/mo. QuarterlyTax Pro: $6/mo."

## Onboarding
Users arrive AFTER experiencing the free calculator. Pricing reinforces value already felt. If arriving from upsell strip, Pro card auto-highlights expense tracking.
