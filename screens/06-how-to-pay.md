# Screen 6: How to Pay Guide

**Route:** `/how-to-pay`
**Purpose:** Eliminate the dead-end after calculation. Answer "what do I do with this number?"
**Emotional Target:** Empowerment. "Now I know how to do this myself."
**Primary Action:** Click through to IRS Direct Pay.

## Layout

Static content page. Single column, max-width 640px.

- **Headline:** "How to Actually Pay Your Quarterly Taxes" — 32px.
- **Intro:** "You've calculated your estimated payment. Here's how to send it to the IRS and your state."

### Federal Payment Section
Three options as cards:
1. **IRS Direct Pay** (free, recommended) — link to portal + step-by-step
2. **EFTPS** (Electronic Federal Tax Payment System) — link + setup instructions
3. **Pay by check/mail** — mailing address + voucher info

### State Payment Section
Dynamic based on user's state (or state from last calculation):
- State tax authority name
- Portal link
- Any state-specific notes
- For coming-soon states: "State payment info for [State] will be added when state tax estimates go live."

### Deadline Reminder
Next deadline date + "Set a Reminder" CTA.
